Cheesecake Recipe - Lemon

This recipe makes extra, since you will want to have some left over for drizzling on desserts, yogurt, or ice cream.

Recommended crust: crushed coconut biscuits with macadamia nuts

Instructions

  1. Whisk together the sugar and eggs in a small pot. This will break the protein bonds in the eggs, helping them not to curdle.
  2. Whisk in lemon juice, zest, and add the butter.
  3. Set on medium heat and stir the butter in as it melts. Stir frequently to stop any curdling.
  4. The curd is finished once it reaches a low boil, and will coat the back of a spoon without sliding off.
  5. For a smoother curd or if the eggs curdled, push through a fine mesh sieve while still hot.
  6. Poor into a glass jar and spread on top of the pre-chilled cheesecake when the curd is skin-warm. Place any extra into the frigde.
  7. Chill curd on lemon cheesecake for at least 30 minutes before serving.
  8. Serve with a dollop of whipped cream on each slice to cut the sour of the lemon.
