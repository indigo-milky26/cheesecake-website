Cheesecake Recipe - Chocolate Caramel Brownie

These recipes may make extra sauce, but you will want to have some left over for drizzling on desserts, yogurt, or ice cream.

Recommended crust: chocolate oreo crust with pecan or walnut

Ingredients

Chocolate Ganache

Brownies

Caramel Drizzle

Instructions

Chocolate Ganache

Double boiler method:

  1. Set cream and chocolate over a double boiler on medium heat. Stir occasionally until combined.
  2. Place in a glass jar and spread on top of pre-chilled cheesecake when skin-warm.

Microwave method:

  1. Microwave whipping cream in a glass or ceramic microwave safe bowl or jar in 30 second intervals until nearly boiling. Keep an eye on it so it does not boil over.
  2. Mix the chocolate with the hot cream and stir until combined. Microwave again in 30 second intervals, stirring between, if chocolate is not fully melted in.
  3. Place in a glass jar and spread on top of pre-chilled cheesecake when skin-warm.

Brownies

  1. After baking and cooling your brownies, cut them into 2 inch (4cm) squares, then cut each in half to make triangles.
  2. Arrange 8 or 10 (depending on how many slices you want) of the prettiest triangles around the top of the cake on top the freshly spread ganache.
  3. Chill the cake in the fridge for at least an hour to set the ganache and brownies in place.

Caramel

  1. Melt the butter in a small pan on medium and cook until it is lightly browned, being careful not to burn it.
  2. Add the sugar, and stir until melted into the butter.
  3. Finally, add the whipping cream and stir until smooth and combined, then remove from the heat.
  4. If the oil beggins to seperate, add 1/2 tsp at a time of cold water and stir vigorously.
  5. Poor the sauce into a glass jar, and let it cool to room temperature.
  6. Finally, drizzle the caramel sauce in long lines (or however you want) across the chilled brownie cheesecake.
  7. Let the caramel set in the fridge for about 10 minutes, then serve and enjoy.
