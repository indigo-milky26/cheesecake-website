Cheesecake Recipe

For one single pie (8-10 servings). 9 inch (23cm) pie plate.

Ingredients

Crust

Cake

Instructions

Crust

  1. Preheat oven to 325°F (165°C).
  2. Using a fork, mix your crushed nuts and crackers together, and combine with melted butter, water and vanilla.
  3. Grease your pie plate. Pack the crust into the bottom and sides and place into preheated oven for 8-10 minutes.

Cake

  1. Beat the sour cream and cream cheese together until smooth.
  2. Add the eggs, mixing in one at a time, next add the sugar, then the vanilla and lemon juice (if using).
  3. Pour the batter into the cooled crust, and bake for 22-25 minutes, checking for the centre to be just slightly jiggly. The cake will set fully as it cools.
  4. Cool the cake completely before setting in the fridge to chill for at least 4 hours or overnight.
  5. After chilling your cake, make your topping of choice: lemon curd, berry, or chocolate caramel brownie.
