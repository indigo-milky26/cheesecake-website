Cheesecake Recipe - (sugar-free) Keto Chocolate Pecan Cheesecake

For one single pie (8-10 servings). 9 inch (23cm) pie plate.

A delicious dessert even for those not on low-carb diets. This is an easy, healthy recipe that doesn't lack flavour and doesn't even taste sugar-free.

Instructions

Cheesecake Base

  1. Preheat oven to 325°F (165°C).
  2. Beat the sour cream and cream cheese together until smooth.
  3. Add the eggs, mixing in one at a time, add the sweetners, then add the vanilla and cinnamon.
  4. Pour the batter into a greased pie plate, and bake for 20-24 minutes, checking for the centre to be just slightly jiggly. The cake will set fully as it cools.
  5. Let it cool completely before adding the topping.

Chocolate Topping

  1. Preheat oven to 325°F (165°C).
  2. Chop your pecans into small chunks, and toast on medium in a non-stick or cast iron frying pan, occasionally stirring for 15 minutes or until aromatic. Set aside to cool.
  3. Place you chocolate squares and a few tablespoons of your sour cream into a microwave safe bowl and microwave in 30 second intervals, stiring until the chocolate is completely melted in.
  4. Mix this in well with 1/4 cup more of the sour cream, then combine with the rest of it.
  5. Mix in you cocoa powder, sweetners, cinnamon and vanilla.
  6. Spread evenly over your cooled cheesecake base, then carefully sprinkle your toasted pecans across the top.
  7. Bake for 20 minutes. Let it cool before placing into the fridge to chill for at least 4 hours or overnight.

Caramel Sauce

  1. Melt the butter in a small pan on medium and cook until it is lightly browned, being careful not to burn it.
  2. Add the sweetner and stir until melted into the butter. This caramel may be grittier than sugar caramel at this stage.
  3. Finally, add the whipping cream and stir until smooth and combined, then remove from the heat.
  4. If the oil beggins to seperate, add 1/2 tsp at a time of cold water and stir vigorously.
  5. Poor the sauce into a glass jar, and let it cool to skin-warm.
  6. Finally, drizzle the caramel sauce in long lines (or however you want) across the chilled cheesecake.
  7. Let the caramel set in the fridge for about 10 minutes, then serve and enjoy.
