Cheesecake Recipe - Layered Pumpkin Cheesecake

If you like pumpkin pie and cheesecake, you will like this. For one pie (8-10 servings). 9 inch (23cm) pie plate.

Recommended crusts: plain, walnut, or pecan (regardless, add 1/2 tsp pumpkin spice to crust)

Ingredients

Pumpkin Layer

Cheesecake Layer

Instructions

Pumpkin Layer

  1. Preheat oven to 325°F (165°C).
  2. Make crust according to the original recipe, adding 1/2 tsp pumpkin spice.
  3. Combine the pumpkin layer ingredients in a bowl and stir well.
  4. Poor the filling into the cooled crust and bake for 20-25 minutes.
  5. Cool it down slightly before adding the cheesecake layer.

Cheesecake Layer

  1. Beat the sour cream and cream cheese together until smooth.
  2. Add the egg and vanilla, mixing in well, then mix in the sugar.
  3. Pour the batter gently across the warm pumpkin layer, and bake for another 20-25 minutes, checking for the centre to be just slightly jiggly. The cake will set fully as it cools.
  4. Cool the cake completely before setting in the fridge to chill for at least 4 hours or overnight.
  5. To serve, top with unsweetened whipped cream on each slice.
