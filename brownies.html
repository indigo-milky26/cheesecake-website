Fudge Brownie Recipe

For one 8 inch (20cm) x 8 inch square pan.

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 325°F (165°C). Spray or grease your pan with butter.
  2. Place the butter on medium heat in a medium saucepan, and turn off the heat when it's melted.
  3. Stir in the sugars, then the vanilla, salt, and cocoa powder.
  4. Set aside to cool until the batter is cool enough not to burn, around 10 minutes.
  5. Pre-crack the eggs in a small bowl, and add in one at a time, beating them in vigorously.
  6. When it is mixed well, add the flour, stirring until incorporated, then stir in the nuts or chocolate chips.
  7. Bake for 25-30 minutes. Remove when they look almost baked, and a toothpick inserted in the egde comes out clean, while one in the centre comes out almost clean. They will continue cooking once taken out.
  8. Let the brownies cool completely in the pan before cutting with a sharp knife, or chill in the fridge for a few hours after cooling for cleanest egdes.
  9. If using on a chocolate brownie cheesecake, bake the brownies when you bake the cheesecake base, and for chill for 4 hours or overnight, cutting and arranging on the ganache the next day.
