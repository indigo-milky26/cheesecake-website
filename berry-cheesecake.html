Instructions

  1. Mix together the cold water and corn starch, making sure no lumps remain.
  2. Mix all ingredients except the cornstarch slurry together in a small pot, set on medium heat.
  3. When it starts to simmer, add in slurry and stir to ensure no lumps form.
  4. The sauce is finished once it reaches a gel-like consistency, and will coat the back of a spoon without dripping off.
  5. Poor into a glass jar and spread on top of the pre-chilled cheesecake when the sauce is skin-warm. Place any extra into the fridge.
  6. Chill sauce on cheesecake for at least 1 hour before serving.
  7. Serve with a dollop of whipped cream on each slice.
